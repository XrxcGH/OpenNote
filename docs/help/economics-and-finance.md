# Economics and finance

One window holds 87 calculators in eight subjects, named Economics and finance. Each one shows its fields, a headline answer, numbered steps you can follow by hand, and a graph where one helps. "Insert answer" and "Insert with steps" add the result to your page. These tools are new in Beta, so check answers against your own work while you learn them.

To open it, use the command palette (Ctrl+K) or the Insert tab's math group, then pick a subject and a calculator.

## Money over time

- Single sums, annuities, perpetuities, interest rates (nominal, effective, continuous), loans with amortization schedules, and savings goals.
- Net present value, internal rate of return, modified IRR, and payback; depreciation including MACRS; real-estate returns; cost-effectiveness (ICER and net monetary benefit).

## Markets and welfare

- Supply and demand equilibrium, consumer and producer surplus, elasticities, taxes and subsidies, price controls and quotas, trade with tariffs, externalities, and the labor market with a monopsony. Graphs shade each area and give its value.

## Firms and strategy

- Monopoly, price discrimination, Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg, collusion, and the Herfindahl-Hirschman index.

## Consumers and costs

- Utility maximization, Slutsky decomposition with compensating and equivalent variation, cost minimization, short-run costs and supply, and two-period consumption.

## Macroeconomics

- Keynesian cross, IS-LM, the Solow model, growth accounting, Taylor rule, Phillips curve, quantity theory, money multiplier, Fisher equation, covered interest parity, purchasing power parity, and difference equations such as the cobweb.

## Measurement

- Price indexes, rebasing and chaining, the GDP deflator, and inflation adjustment with the bundled U.S. CPI-U table (Bureau of Labor Statistics, public domain).
- Growth rates, inequality (Gini, Lorenz curve, Theil, Atkinson, Palma), poverty, segregation, location quotients, shift-share, Leontief input-output, the Human Development Index, and income taxes from typed brackets or the 2024 U.S. federal schedule.

## Accounting

- Journal entries to statements, inventory (FIFO, LIFO, average cost), ratios and DuPont, cost-volume-profit, variances, process costing, activity-based costing, bonds payable, audit analytics (Benford, attribute sampling), and unit economics.
- In journal fields, separate entries with a vertical bar.

## Investments

- Bond price, yield, duration, and convexity; yield-curve bootstrapping; returns; CAPM; portfolios (minimum variance and tangency); Black-Scholes with Greeks; binomial trees; strategy payoffs; simulated price paths.
- Cost of capital, discounted cash flow with sensitivity, the Gordon model, funding rounds and dilution, SAFEs, and the venture method.

These are study aids, not financial advice.
