# Photos from your phone

Send photos from your phone straight onto the page you have open, with no app to install and no account. It's handy for a whiteboard, a page of a book, or a receipt.

## Send photos

1. Open the page the photos should go on.
2. Choose **Insert**, then **From phone**. OpenNote shows a QR code.
3. Point your phone's camera at the code and open the link it finds.
4. On your phone, choose **Take a photo** or **Choose photos**. Each photo lands on the page as soon as it arrives.
5. Choose **Done** on your PC when you have what you need.

Your phone and your PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. A phone using mobile data can't reach the PC.

## What OpenNote does to keep this private

- OpenNote listens on your home or work network only while the **From phone** window is open, and for 10 minutes at most. Closing the window stops it at once.
- The code works once. The phone that opens it is the only one that can send photos, until the window closes.
- After 10 wrong codes, OpenNote stops listening. Choose **Show a new code** to go on.
- Only JPEG and PNG photos come in, each up to 20 MB, and up to 30 for each code. OpenNote reads each photo with size limits, turns it the right way up, and saves a fresh copy. That copy leaves out what the camera stored about the photo, such as where you took it.
- The connection is encrypted with a certificate made for this one session. Your phone's browser may warn that it doesn't know the certificate, because no company signed it. If you want to check it, compare the fingerprint in the browser's certificate details with the one OpenNote shows under the code.

## Tidy up a photo

Scan cleanup straightens a photo of a page and evens out its lighting. Select the photo on the page, then choose Clean up scan from the command palette. Photos from your phone don't clean themselves up as they arrive. See [Import and export](import-and-export.md).

From phone is on in Beta builds.
