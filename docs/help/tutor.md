# Tutor

The tutor helps when you are stuck, without giving the answer away. It gives hints one small step at a time, and it explains a step that Check my work flagged. It runs on this device and reads only the text you show it.

## Turn it on

The tutor is off until you turn it on. The first time you use a tutor command, it asks. You can also open Settings, then Editing, and turn on Tutor.

## Get a hint

1. Select the problem and your working: lasso your handwriting, or select typed lines. With nothing selected, the tutor takes the math lines of the page.
2. Press Ctrl+K and choose Give me a hint, or right-click the selected text and choose Give me a hint.
3. The Tutor panel opens at the right with the first hint. Press Next hint for more.

Each problem has four hints:

1. Where to look.
2. The idea or rule involved.
3. A question about the next step.
4. The next step itself.

After you try, Show the full solution shows the whole working. It asks first, because working it out yourself helps it stick.

## Explain a flagged step

Run Check my work. In its card, press Explain this step beside a line that needs a look. The tutor says what went wrong and which rule applies, and leaves the fix to you. Ask for a hint to go on.

## The Tutor panel

Press Ctrl+K and choose Open the tutor. The panel shows What the tutor sees. You can edit that text, take the selected lines with Use selected lines, or clear it. Type a question in Ask the tutor and press Send. The conversation stays while the panel is open, also on other pages. Closing the panel forgets it.

The panel works with a keyboard, a pen, touch, and a screen reader. Escape closes it.

## Where the tutor runs

- With no download, the tutor uses its built-in hints. They cover the slips Check my work finds, equations in one letter, and derivatives, integrals, limits, and sums. Other subjects get guiding questions.
- With a language model downloaded (Settings, then On-device intelligence), the model writes the hints on this device.
- With your own key for a cloud service (Settings, then Editing, under Tutor), the panel first shows the text it would send and where. Press Send for this session to agree, or Keep it on this device. A yes also covers your next hints on the same text and what you type in Ask the tutor, until you close the panel. New text asks again.

While Work offline is on, nothing is sent, and the tutor stays on this device. A hint that would give away the answer is replaced with a built-in hint.
