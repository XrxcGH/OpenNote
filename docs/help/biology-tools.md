# Biology tools

Work molecular biology, genetics, and evolution problems with worked steps, pictures, and tables you can put on the page. Everything works offline and keeps your work on this computer. The tools are for coursework and lab planning, not for clinical or diagnostic use.

Open the window from the command palette with **Open the biology tools**, or open one tool directly by its name, such as **Phylogenetic trees**. Each tab answers as you type. **Insert answer**, **Insert equation**, **Insert results table**, and **Insert steps** put that part at the caret, and each picture and table has its own Insert button. **Reset to the example** brings back the worked example.

## Molecular biology

- **DNA and RNA sequences.** Clean, complement, translate, and count a sequence, find open reading frames, motifs, GC windows, and CRISPR guides, and describe mutations.
- **Cloning, PCR, and gels.** Cut a linear or circular sequence with 56 common enzymes or your own, see the fragments on a virtual gel and a plasmid map, size bands from a ladder, report a primer's melting temperatures by the Wallace, salt-adjusted, and nearest-neighbor methods, find a PCR product with an annealing temperature, and work out oligo concentrations, ligation amounts, and copy numbers.
- **Proteins and peptides.** Mass, isoelectric point, charge curves, extinction coefficients, and hydropathy plots of a protein sequence.
- **Sequence alignment.** Global, local, and semi-global alignment of 2 DNA or protein sequences with the score matrix, and dot plots.

## Genetics

- **Genetic crosses and linkage.** Punnett squares for up to 3 genes, ratios and probabilities, chi-square tests, gene maps from 2- and 3-point crosses, LOD scores, and carrier risk.
- **Population genetics.** Allele frequencies and Hardy-Weinberg tests, selection, mutation, migration, seeded drift runs, effective size, linkage disequilibrium, and heritability.
- **Family, pedigree, and kinship charts.** Type a family as a list to draw a pedigree in the standard symbols, check which inheritance patterns fit, work genotype chances, sum relationship coefficients, read kin types such as FBD from Ego, list a line of succession, or draw a genogram.

## Evolution

- **Phylogenetic trees.** Build trees from an alignment or a distance matrix by UPGMA or neighbor-joining with every matrix shown, score and search trees by parsimony, add seeded bootstrap support, and read, reroot, and ask questions of Newick trees.

## Good to know

- Seeded tools (drift and bootstrap) show their seed. The same seed gives the same result on any computer.
- Reference values name their source under the result, such as the SantaLucia (1998) nearest-neighbor parameters.
- The pattern check in the pedigree tool assumes one gene and no new mutations. Choose incomplete penetrance to let dominant traits skip a generation.
